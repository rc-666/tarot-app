Who we are

[App name] is a free tarot app for Android. The data controller is [company name, address]. Contact: hello@example.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Your first name, to greet you in the app.
  • Your birth date, and if you choose, birth time and birth place, to work out your sign and your daily cards.
  • Your email and a password, so your profile is saved and you can sign in on a new phone. We store only a secure hash of the password.
  • Your focus topics, language, readings and ratings, to show your reading and improve the texts.
  • App usage events (for example "reading opened"), with PostHog, to see what works. We never send your birth details or email with these events.
  • An advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to show the banner ads that keep the app free.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your birth details for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your profile is stored with Supabase (data centre in São Paulo, Brazil). Analytics are processed by PostHog and ads by Google AdMob, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, birth details, readings and ratings are deleted from our database straight away. Backups are overwritten within [30] days.

Your rights

You can see and change your details in the app (Settings → Edit profile), and delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete account, or see Delete my account). Under Brazil's LGPD you can also ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or to stop processing it: write to hello@example.com.

Children

The app is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account.
